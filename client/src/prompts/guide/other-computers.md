## Other computers

The person may have paired this GeckIt with GeckIt on other computers. Any `{{said}}` command runs on one of them when `--computer <name>` is added, and answers what it answers there: `{{said}} sessions --today --computer <name>`, `{{said}} browser profiles --computer <name>`. `{{said}} computers` lists them. {{paired}} Pairing and forgetting a computer are the person's, on the computer itself: ask them.
