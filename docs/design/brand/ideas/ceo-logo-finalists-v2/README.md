# GeckIt logo finalists, v2

Revision of [ceo-logo-finalists](../ceo-logo-finalists/README.md) after the Lebedev-standard review. `python3 build.py` regenerates every SVG and the [comparison sheet](geckit-finalists-v2.png).

| Change | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Wordmark replaced by the approved round-14 wordmark, k and I separate | The fused k-I bar read as "GeckHt" at 16 px (tests 1 and 8) |
| `joint`: the original geometry, unchanged | Reference |
| `joint-wide`: right piece 10 units wider, left piece shifted left, nothing else changed | The narrow right piece plus tab read as the letters "CH" at 16 px (test 3) |
| `handoff`: larger card tilted 12 degrees with a cut corner, gap to the hand doubled | At 16 px the fold vanished and card and hand merged (test 2) |
| `imprint`: round knob on a neck above the base | The flat handle read as a hat at 16 px (test 3) |

Open: `joint` metaphor fits any integration product (test 4); `handoff` is close to stock "hand holding" pictograms (test 3); `imprint` signals bureaucracy to startup CEOs (test 10); green is shared with Tasklet (test 10). Drawing does not fix these.
