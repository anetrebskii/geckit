# GeckIt: own logo directions

**Task.** A mark that tells a startup CEO that GeckIt turns their Codex and Claude Code conversations into employees they hand work to. The product is free, the audience pays for professional tools.

**Category.** The saved sample of 15 marks ([sheet](../round-11-symbol/geckit-competitors.png)) repeats abstract geometric tiles, letter monograms, faces and mascots, and violet, green and orange. Rejected earlier in this repo: badge, joint, hand, stamp, chair, lever, rail switch, tie and suit, org chart, @ with gecko, kanban, card stack, nameplate, compass, cursor arm.

**Principle.** One everyday object from GeckIt's own work that a CEO recognises without a caption. Green marks the AI employee's part only.

[Comparison sheet](geckit-own-ideas.png) · `python3 build.py` regenerates it. Wordmark: the approved round-14 XCharter Bold.

| Direction | Idea | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0. Chat with a check (first sketch) | Task done in chat | Discarded: the stock "message delivered" pictogram |
| 1. Night shift | Dark office tower, one lit window: the AI keeps working after everyone left | Dropped: at 16 px a dark slab with a dot reads as a door or a phone (tests 2, 3, 4) |
| 2. Order ticket | A kitchen ticket on the rail, as a task on the board | Dropped: the first version formed a green T like Tasklet; tilted, it blurs at 16 px (test 2) and needs a caption (test 4) |
| **3. Hired chat** | **The chat bubble is the head, green shoulders with a collar are the body: the conversation got hired** | **Recommended** |

## Tests: Hired chat

Rendered at 16, 32 and 512 px, one colour and reversed, mark and lockup.

| # | Test | Result |

| 1 | Legibility | Passed: the lockup reads GeckIt at 16 px |

| 3 | Looks like | Passed with a risk. Closest found: BrandCrowd templates "Head Speech Bubble" (profile head outlined as a bubble) and "Human Chat Message" (profile head inside a bubble pin); both are side-view heads without a body, ours is a front bubble on shoulders with a collar. Risk: in a round avatar at 24-32 px it is close to the default "no photo" person silhouette. Not a trademark search |
| 4 | Meaning | Passed: "The conversation got hired" |
| 5 | One idea | Passed |
| 6 | Filler | Passed: bubble, tail and collar each carry the idea |
| 7 | Originality | Passed within the sample; wider search is test 3 |
| 8 | Type | Passed: solid mark matches the weight of XCharter Bold |
| 9 | Drawing | Passed: the tail doubles as the neck, shoulders symmetric |
| 10 | Fit | Colour: green is shared with Tasklet (open since round 11). Price: business collar fits the CEO audience. Breaks with the G mark, as asked |
