# Already on it

A wordmark for GeckIt, the free AI workspace for startup CEOs. The aim is to suggest how quickly a team can put a brief in motion. The design uses a change of pace inside the name: Geck stands upright, It leans forward 16 degrees and both stay on one baseline.

## Review against Lebedev Logo

| Test | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Legibility | The name reads GeckIt at 16 px high. The two serifs distinguish the capital I. |
| 2. Size | The full name and G survive at 16 and 32 px. The cut ends are most evident at 32 px and above. |
| 3. Resemblance | The small mark shares Grammarly's initial and green colour family. The serif outline, open dark field and forward lean distinguish the drawing, but the initial alone is a weak identifier. |
| 4. Meaning | The pace changes on the word It. The headline supplies the connection to a brief already being worked on. CEO interpretation has not been tested. |
| 5. One idea | Motion comes from one typographic change across the name. |
| 6. Filler | The primary logo uses only the six letters. |
| 7. Originality | Oblique lettering is a familiar signal for speed. Switching at It is specific to this name. The G alone loses that distinction. |
| 8. Type | Outlines derive from the project's XCharter Bold. The end of the G crossbar and the I and t terminals were redrawn; the gap after k separates the upright and oblique forms. |
| 9. Drawing | Baseline and cap height stay aligned in the two treatments. |
| 10. Fit | Retains the established name, type and ink colour. The optional green icon fails strict colour exclusivity against the saved category sample. |

The mark and wordmark were rendered in ink and white at 16, 32 and 512 px. The app and social images are concept previews. Recognition of the icon on its own remains unverified. Type source and licence are in the project brand kit.
