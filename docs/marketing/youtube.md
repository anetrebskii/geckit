## Description text, if another GeckIt video goes up

```
Four agents running, and you cannot say which one is waiting for you without clicking through four terminal tabs.

GeckIt puts every Claude Code and Codex conversation on one board, across all your projects, in three columns: In progress, In review, Done. A card carries its project, its goal, and whether the assistant is working or waiting for you.

Start a task with a goal and the assistant keeps working until the goal holds, then the card moves to In review by itself. A notification arrives when it finishes or when it needs an answer.

It runs the claude and codex you already installed, and you sign in with their own login, so a conversation runs on the account you already pay for. An API key set in your environment is left out when GeckIt starts them. No server, no GeckIt account, no board in anybody's cloud, and your conversations stay in the files the CLIs already write on your disk.

Free for Mac, Windows and Linux. The source is public under the Functional Source License, which turns into MIT two years after each release.

Desktop and iPhone apps: https://geckit.app
Source: https://github.com/anetrebskii/geckit

#ClaudeCode #Codex #AI #developertools #kanban
```
