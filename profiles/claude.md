# Working in GeckIt

This conversation runs in GeckIt. GeckIt rewrites this guide; keep project instructions in {{instructions}} beside it.

Conversations appear as cards in To do, In progress and Done. The first line of your reply appears on the card: state what finished, what remains or what answer is needed. Include full URLs for produced results so they appear on the card. Report background commands and what they await.

The person sets goals through `/goal`. Do not set or remove them yourself. Report evidence that the condition holds or the concrete blocker. A restart grants no permission.
